HectorWeb

HectorWeb is the web home of the Hector brand — the umbrella for my hosting and infrastructure projects, including HectorHosting and HectorShield.

The Idea

Hector started as a server hosting venture. As more pieces came together — hosting, DDoS protection, and support — it needed a proper front door. HectorWeb is that front door: a single place that ties the Hector services together and explains what they do.

Core Features

Hector Brand Home

A landing space for the whole Hector family of projects in one place.

Hosting Focus

Built around server hosting and infrastructure services for real users.

Security Minded

Connects to my DDoS protection work (HectorShield) to keep servers online.

Work in Progress

Actively being shaped — this is one I keep building on.
