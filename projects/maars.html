Maars

Maars is a premium footwear store — an authorized retailer concept for Nike, Adidas and Puma releases, curating athletic and lifestyle sneakers with worldwide express shipping.

The Idea

Sneaker drops move fast and most storefronts feel slow and cluttered. I wanted to build a clean, editorial store where the shoes are the focus: strong imagery, a tidy catalogue, and a checkout that doesn't get in the way. I prototyped the concept with Google AI Studio and shipped it as a fast TypeScript web app.

Core Features

Curated Catalogue

Hand-picked athletic and lifestyle silhouettes alongside limited-edition releases.

Fast Storefront

Built for quick browsing with smooth product galleries and minimal friction.

AI-Assisted Build

Prototyped with Google AI Studio, then refined by hand in TypeScript.

Responsive Design

Looks right on everything from a phone in your pocket to a wide desktop.

Tech Stack Breakdown

  • Frontend: TypeScript + Vite for a fast, typed build.
  • Built with: Google AI Studio (Gemini) during prototyping.
  • Design: a clean editorial storefront layout focused on product imagery.
  • Live: maars-psi.vercel.app
  • Repository: github.com/anshalmanoj/maars

Looking Forward

Next steps are a real cart and checkout flow, product filtering by brand and size, and a proper inventory backend so the store can run for real.
